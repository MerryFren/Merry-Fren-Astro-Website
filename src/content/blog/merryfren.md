## What we do
Merry Fren is a community on Robinhood Chain. The token is $FREN. People trade it. Trading creates fees. Those fees are used to buy Bitcoin. That Bitcoin goes to eligible holders.
No staking. No signup. Hold $FREN.
Robin Hood had Merry Men. Robinhood Chain has Merry Fren. The old story gave gold to the people. This one gives Bitcoin.

## What’s in the works

The fee-to-Bitcoin loop is the live idea. A deflationary buy-and-burn is planned. More token utility is planned. A small NFT collection is coming for early frens. We are talking to other projects and possible partners on the chain. Nothing extra is live until we say so on merryfren.com and @MerryFren.

## Why it’s different

Robinhood Chain put stocks, gold, and memecoins on the same rail. A lot of tokens on that chain pair with Nvidia, Apple, gold, or another name. Hold the meme, get a slice of that asset.
Merry Fren uses the same pattern and points it at Bitcoin. Fixed supply. One billion. No extra tokens to pay the story. Activity is supposed to buy the hardest asset on the network, not print more of itself.

## Why Bitcoin, and why stack it

Bitcoin is the scarcest widely held money that runs without a company, a board, or a mint. Cap is known. Issuance falls on a schedule. Nobody can vote a new billion into existence.
Stocks are claims on firms. Gold is old and heavy. Fiat is whoever prints it. Bitcoin is the asset you can hold, move, and verify without asking permission. That is what “hard” means here: costly to produce, costly to dilute, easy to keep.

If a memecoin is going to pay holders anything, the honest unit is Bitcoin. That is the stack. $FREN is how this community tries to turn its own trading into more of that stack.
